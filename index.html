Operator reference

How do I begin?

Let PARADIGM-OS finish booting, then open the Gateway Transmission for a guided first pass. The Seven Seals case file contains the main sequence.

How is archive clearance earned?

Clearance advances through the Seven Seals. Records above your current level remain sealed until the corresponding work is complete.

How do I search the records?

Use the archive search control or press /. Search covers indexed records, personnel, stations and programmes.

Why are some records incomplete?

The restoration ledger tracks recovered, reindexed and missing material. Some source files and cross-references have not been recovered.

Where is my operator progress stored?

Progress is stored in this browser only. No account is required, and information entered in local forms is not transmitted or retained.
